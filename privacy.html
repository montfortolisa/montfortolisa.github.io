MedYield Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MedYield is a question bank for medical students. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. MedYield does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect, send or sell any personal information. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (your answers, flagged questions, notes, flashcards you make, flashcard review dates, time spent and settings) is saved only in your phone's or browser's own storage, so that you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Ask AI

If you tap Ask AI, MedYield copies the question text (never anything about you) and opens the AI app you choose. What happens there is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Internet use

The app downloads its files and display fonts when first opened. After that, it works offline. No information about you is sent when this happens.

Children

MedYield is intended for medical students and other adult learners. It is not directed at children.

Medical disclaimer

MedYield is for education and exam preparation only. It is not medical advice and must not be used to diagnose or treat any patient.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
